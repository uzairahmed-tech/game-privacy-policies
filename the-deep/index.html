Privacy Policy for The Deep

Last updated: 6 November 2026

The Deep is a game made by BiznisDigital for children aged 9 to 12. This policy explains what information the game uses.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store or share any personal information. The game has no accounts, no sign-in, no chat and no way to send messages or photos. Your progress, such as coins, upgrades and missions, is saved only on your device and is deleted when the game is uninstalled.

Advertising

The game shows occasional full-screen ads between dives, provided by Google AdMob. Every ad request is marked as directed at children and under the age of consent, limited to content rated G, and set to non-personalised ads. The game does not use the advertising ID.

To show ads, Google may process limited technical information such as the device type, operating system version and an approximate location derived from the IP address, as described in Google's advertising policies and the AdMob children's privacy guidelines. This information is not used to build a profile of the player.

Children's privacy

The game is designed to follow the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the EU GDPR as it applies to children, and the Google Play Families policy. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Permissions

The game uses internet access to load ads and vibration for game feedback. It does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, photos or precise location.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with an updated date.

Contact

Parents or guardians with questions can contact us at info@biznisdigital.com.
